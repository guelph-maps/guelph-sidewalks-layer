The City layer is accurate to 1–5 m, and 0.2 km of the untraced length sits 15–25 m from an OSM line that no other City sidewalk matches, so the honest figure is ~0–5 km; 0.1 km has nothing within 50 m. The original audit's “OSM has 92%” divided total lengths — it never asked whether they are the same sidewalks.

Each City sidewalk is sampled every 5 m; a sample counts as traced when OSM has a pedestrian line (or a road tagged sidewalk=*) within 15 m, and a segment ≥ 70% traced is matched. Streets are named after the nearest OSM road, so their count may differ slightly from the audit's 97, which used City centrelines. Click a street to zoom to it and list its segments; click a segment (or a line on the map) to select it, then press O to open the spot on openstreetmap.org, I in the iD editor or J in a running JOSM through remote control (each segment has the same OSM · iD · JOSM links). Cyan lines from z15 are the City's own layer; check current imagery before adding anything.
